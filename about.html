HELLO!

I'm Mertcan. When approaching design, I see interfaces not just as clickable screens, but as bridges built between people and technology. I use the analytical perspective from my industrial design background to craft UX/UI experiences that make life easier in the digital world.

I want to design AI-driven products that are actually easy for everyday users to navigate. My focus is on turning complex technology into intuitive, frictionless interfaces making human-AI interaction feel natural and effortless

When I step away from the design desk, you can catch me stepping onto the stage as an actor and vocalist in musicals, capturing moments through photography, or diving deep into the details of coffee brewing techniques.
