Project Goals & Brief

Brief

Designing a digital solution that helps
remote working teams collaborate, communicate, or engage with each other in a more meaningful way.

It is expected to think beyond productivity tools and explore how digital experiences can support trust, belonging, creativity and everyday team interaction.

Problem Statement

It is hard to establish sustainable bonds and maintain visibility with the loss of natural office interactions. This situation creates a need for a need for low-pressure and spontaneous socialization throughout the entire journey.

Research Overview

Desk Research

  • Literature Review
  • Industry Benchmarking
  • Workplace pattern mapping

Survey Overviews

  • Conducted a survey with 28 participants.
  • Google Forms was the tool used

Interview Overviews

  • Conducted semi-structured interviews with 5 Commenis designers from different projects.

Overall Research Insights

Spontaneous Interaction

%78

Miss spontaneous water-cooler interactions the most.

Small Group First

%58

prefer micro-gatherings (2–3 people) over crowded events.

Interest-Driven Connection:

Cold interactions fail; organic bonding requires common hobbies, topics, or automated facilitation.

Lack of Adaptation

Without structured re-engagement, a divide remains between original teams and new hires.

Cross-Team Talks

%66

struggle with losing informal touchpoints with other teams.

Usability Test

In line with the feedback we received from our design mentors at Commencis, we revised our pages using Figma.
Then vibe-coded them with Codex and published the URL. Thanks to this, we also had the chance to conduct remote usability testing.

Moderated Tests

We carried out our qualitative, moderated study using the "think-aloud" protocol, both online (via Teams) and face-to-face.

Structure

We asked our participants 4 warm-up questions, 5 main task scenarios, and 7 closing questions.

Test of Key Features

Socialization tools, homepage discovery, participation in breakout rooms and communities, the podcast finding process, new room creation, and the "Coffee Roulette" feature.
